User authentication is the process of verifying a person's identity to ensure they are who they claim to be before granting access to a service, such as Google Drive [1, 2].

Based on the provided sign-in interface, authentication involves several key components:

  • Identification: The user must provide a unique identifier, typically an email address or phone number, to begin the process [1, 2].
  • Account Recovery: Systems often include safeguards for lost credentials, such as a "Forgot email?" link to help users regain access to their accounts [1, 2].
  • Privacy and Security Measures: When using public or shared computers, users are encouraged to use private browsing windows or Guest mode to prevent their credentials from being saved [1, 2].
  • Legal and Policy Framework: The authentication process is governed by specific Privacy policies and Terms of service, which are made available to the user during sign-in [1, 2].
  • Onboarding: For individuals without an existing identity in the system, there is a clear path to "Create account" [1, 2].
