Frequently asked questions

What is the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for iPaaS?
  • Gartner defines integration platform as a service (iPaaS) as a vendor-managed cloud service that enables end users to implement integrations between a variety of applications, services and data sources.
Why is the Magic Quadrant™ for iPaaS important?
  • If you’re evaluating iPaaS solutions, we believe, the Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ report can be a critical companion in your search for the perfect fit. Even if you aren’t currently evaluating vendors, the report provides:
    • Expert perspectives about iPaaS vendors
    • Analysis of key differentiators, industry benchmarks, and trends
    • Latest developments in integration technology
How do I interpret the Magic Quadrant™?
  • A Magic Quadrant provides a graphical competitive positioning of four types of technology providers, in markets where growth is high and provider differentiation is distinct:
    • Leaders, who execute well against their current vision for changing market rules
    • Visionaries, who understand where the market is going or have a vision for changing market rules, but do not yet execute well - Niche Players, who focus successfully on a small segment or are unfocused and do not out-innovate or outperform others - Challengers, who execute well today or may dominate a large segment but do not demonstrate an understanding of market direction
